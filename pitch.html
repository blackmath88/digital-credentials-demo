A proposal for modern learning recognition

Make the certificate useful after the course.

Turn a training certificate from a PDF in an inbox into a durable digital credential people can understand, keep, share and — with Open Badges 3.0 — verify independently.

Simple to issue.
Valuable to receive.
Built on an open standard.
The product pitch

The website makes the certificate desirable. Open Badges makes it portable, verifiable and interoperable.

The current experience

A good training often ends with a weak artefact.

01 / Today

PDF by email

A certificate is generated, attached to an email and eventually disappears into a downloads folder.

02 / Participant

Hard to use

Sharing it on LinkedIn, a CV or a personal site requires manual work. Verification usually means contacting the issuer.

03 / Institution

Data is trapped

The achievement, competencies, criteria and issuer information exist mostly as pixels and text rather than reusable learning data.

Why Open Badges 3.0

More than a nice HTML certificate.

Nice HTML certificate

A webpage about an achievement

  • Beautiful and human-readable
  • Shareable link
  • Printable and easy to brand
  • Trust depends mainly on the site displaying it
Open Badges 3.0 credential

A portable record of an achievement

  • Structured, machine-readable achievement data
  • Issuer, recipient, criteria, skills and evidence can travel with it
  • Designed for independent verification
  • Can be displayed by different wallets, sites and systems
  • Supports stackable learning pathways and interoperability

We are not building prettier PDFs. We are giving learning achievements a durable digital identity.

What changes for our certificates?

Keep the institutional weight. Add digital usefulness.

01 / Preserve

The certificate remains beautiful

Typography, signatures, institutional identity and a printable A4 artefact still matter. The physical certificate becomes one rendering of the credential.

02 / Structure

Capture what the learning means

Course title, duration, competencies, criteria, skills, issuer, date and evidence become structured data instead of being trapped in a PDF.

03 / Extend

Give participants useful exits

LinkedIn, CV, website embed, QR, wallet, downloadable PDF and a public verification page come from the same credential record.

Build path

Start with the experience. Add trust underneath.

Bite 1

Desirable artefact

One excellent credential page, print view, sharing, QR, CV embed and structured internal record.

Bite 2

Open Badges 3.0

Map the internal model to the standard and test compatibility against MIT/DCC examples and verifiers.

Bite 3

Real verification

Issuer identity, signing and independent verification. The page is no longer the source of truth — the credential is.

Bite 4

Issuing workflow

Create programmes, upload participants, preview, issue and manage credentials with a deliberately small organiser interface.

The principle

The certificate should not be the end of the learning experience. It should become reusable proof of it.
