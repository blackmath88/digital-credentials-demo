Where this stops being real.

Real: the workflow, the participant parsing, the Open Badges 3.0 mapping, the deterministic credential records, and the fact that any of these records can be signed locally through the Bite 3.1 path.

Simulated: issuance authority, delivery, hosting and credential status. credentialUrl is a placeholder, delivery status is a label rather than a sent message, and no record carries a proof.

Not claimed: that Implement Learning Institute or University of Basel issued, signed or endorsed anything produced here. A production issuer needs an institution-controlled identifier, key custody, an issuance policy and a status/revocation service.
