# Conversation brief — Implement

## One sentence

What happens if the certificate participants receive after training becomes a durable digital credential they can actually use on LinkedIn, a CV or their website?

## Why it may matter

The current paper/PDF certificate already carries the important facts: achievement, issuer, participant, date, duration and learning content. The prototype turns the same information into a useful public artefact while preserving a path to recognised credential standards.

## Demo to show

Show the existing certificate transformed into the public digital experience; use the LinkedIn handoff and CV embed; print/save the certificate view; show the structured record and explain the Open Badges 3.0 path; and be explicit that the current demo is not signed by Implement and should only become a real issuer flow with Implement's agreement.

## Pilot question

Would it be worthwhile to test this with one future cohort, with Implement controlling the issuer identity and approval of the achievement text?

## What a later issuer flow should feel like

Create/select training → upload participant list → review → issue.

The participant experience should stay premium; the administrative experience should stay boring.
