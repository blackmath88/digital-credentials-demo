# Conversation brief — University of Basel L&D

## One sentence

Explore whether selected internal learning achievements could become portable, standards-based digital credentials rather than ending as isolated PDFs or attendance records.

## Why it may matter

The interesting part is not a prettier certificate. A structured credential can connect the participant experience with learning evidence, capability development and later interoperability with wallets, HR/L&D systems or other credential ecosystems.

A deliberately small pilot could test desirability and operational effort before any platform decision.

## Possible University use cases

Leadership and development programmes, internal academies and capability pathways, AI learning / digital-work capability development, Modern Workplace / M365 learning tracks, and cohort programmes where participants benefit from a portable external-facing record.

## Demo to show

Open the public Implement training example; show the participant view, print/PDF, LinkedIn handoff and CV embed; open the structured record; explain that Bite 1 is a representation while Open Badges 3.0 signing/verification is the next technical validation; then ask where a **small internal pilot** would create genuine participant value.

## Decision we want from the conversation

Not procurement. Not a platform programme.

We want permission to identify one plausible University learning use case and validate whether a standards-based credential would be useful to participants and manageable for L&D.
