Why stopping here is important.

A standards-shaped JSON file is not automatically trustworthy. Real verification only begins when the actual issuer controls an identifier and signing key, signs the credential, and publishes enough information for an independent verifier to resolve the proof and status.

So this lab deliberately says structurally mapped, not verified. That boundary is the product's trust model.
